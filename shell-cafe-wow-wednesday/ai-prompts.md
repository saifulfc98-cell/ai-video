# Prompt Jana Video AI

Untuk shot yang tidak sempat dirakam. Prompt dalam Bahasa Inggeris (kebanyakan model video lebih tepat). Semua: **9:16, 1080×1920, 4–5 s, tiada teks/logo dijana AI** — tambah logo & teks sebenar semasa suntingan supaya jenama tidak herot.

> Shot penyampai (1, 4, 5) **paling baik dirakam sebenar**. Jika guna image-to-video dari `assets/presenter-reference.jpg`, dapatkan persetujuan Haslam dahulu dan jangan jana dialog/lip-sync yang dia tidak sebut sendiri.

## Shot 1 / 4 — Penyampai (image-to-video, rujukan foto)
```
Image-to-video from reference photo. Same man, same red and yellow uniform and red cap,
standing at a petrol station forecourt, smiling warmly at camera, holding a small handheld
microphone at chest height, subtle natural head movement and blinking, soft overcast daylight,
shallow depth of field, background trees softly blurred, handheld news-camera feel, 9:16.
```

## Shot 2a — Espresso
```
Extreme close-up of fresh espresso pouring from a portafilter into a white paper cup,
rich golden crema, steam rising, warm café lighting, slow motion, shallow depth of field, 9:16.
```

## Shot 2b — Susu berbuih
```
Close-up of a barista pouring steamed milk into an espresso cup forming latte art,
warm amber counter lights, smooth slow motion, cinematic, 9:16.
```

## Shot 2c — Pelanggan
```
Malaysian commuter in smart casual clothes picking up a takeaway coffee cup from a
convenience store counter and smiling, bright clean modern store interior, natural motion, 9:16.
```

## Shot 3a — Telefon / app
```
Close-up of a hand holding a smartphone over a store payment counter, phone screen shows a
generic QR code, hand moves phone toward a counter scanner, crisp focus, clean lighting, 9:16.
```

## Shot 3b — Pastri
```
Tray of freshly baked golden croissants and pastries in a heated glass display case,
gentle steam and warm glow, slow camera push-in, appetising food cinematography, 9:16.
```

## Shot 5 — Outro
```
A takeaway coffee cup placed gently on a counter by a hand, petrol station canopy lights
glowing in warm bokeh behind at dusk, slow cinematic push-in, empty space at top for title text, 9:16.
```
